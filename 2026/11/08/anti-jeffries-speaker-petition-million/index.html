Petition against Jeffries as speaker passes 1 million signatures, but no rival emerges

November 8, 2026, updated 9:25 AM ET

A Change.org petition titled "House Democrats: CHOOSE A NEW SPEAKER" passed one million verified signatures on Saturday, November 7. It asks every member of the incoming Congress to vote against Hakeem Jeffries for speaker in January 2027, according to Common Dreams and The New York Sun. On November 6, Axios reported that House Democrats had been "inundated" with calls urging them not to back Jeffries, and that several incoming members from deeply red seats would not say firmly how they would vote. Jeffries formally launched his bid on November 4 and remains the clear favorite. As of the latest reporting, no House Democrat has entered the race against him. Rep. Ro Khanna has said he will press Jeffries for policy commitments, including on Iran, military aid to Israel and Medicare for All, but has not announced a challenge.

Why it matters

Democrats won at least 254 seats in last week's midterms, so Jeffries can lose a sizable number of votes and still win the gavel. The grassroots campaign shows how much pressure he faces from the left before the caucus leadership elections later this month.
