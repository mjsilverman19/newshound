Jeffries backs Hegseth impeachment as House Republicans brace for defections

November 8, 2026, updated 8:27 PM ET

On ABC's "This Week" on Sunday, November 8, House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries said he is a "hard yes" on a resolution to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. He accused Hegseth of abusing his power and of engineering the costly war with Iran, and said he expects "incredibly strong support" from Democrats. The same day, Axios reported that House Republican leaders are bracing for enough GOP defections to impeach Hegseth when the measure comes up this week, with Republicans who lost their seats in last Tuesday's midterms seen as having little left to lose. Outgoing Rep. Thomas Massie told ABC he will bring a privileged impeachment resolution as early as Monday, which forces a vote within two legislative days. Hegseth has vowed to fight it, and President Trump has said he intends to keep him, though CBS reported that Trump has been asking advisers whether Hegseth should stay.

Why it matters

Until now the impeachment push had looked like something for the new Democratic House to take up in January. A vote in the lame-duck session this week now looks likely, and it could pass. That would put more pressure on Trump to drop Hegseth before a Senate trial and could open the way to similar efforts against other Cabinet members, such as Robert F. Kennedy Jr.
