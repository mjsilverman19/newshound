Anthropic presses ahead with pre-Thanksgiving IPO as roadshow week begins

November 8, 2026, updated 8:32 PM ET

The Financial Times reported on November 6-7, citing several investors, that Anthropic is going ahead with its US listing this month despite the tech selloff. It plans to raise $80 billion to $100 billion at a valuation of $1.25 trillion to $1.75 trillion, below the $2 trillion investors talked about a month ago. On Sunday, November 8, coverage previewing the week described the company starting its pitch to institutional investors, with a debut targeted before Thanksgiving. No price range or pricing date had been filed, and Anthropic has not confirmed the timetable. This search found no single new trigger on November 8, and nothing that explains the shift in expected release dates for new AI models in the same topic.

Why it matters

Earlier this month the tech selloff raised doubts that the listing would happen on time. The reports that Anthropic will take a lower valuation rather than wait make a listing in the week of November 16-20 look more likely. It would still be one of the largest IPOs on record, though at a smaller valuation than first hoped.
