Trump says Dana White press secretary offer was a joke

November 9, 2026, updated 6:11 PM ET

On Saturday, November 7, President Donald Trump posted on Truth Social that UFC president and CEO Dana White had been offered the job of White House press secretary. On Sunday, November 8, Trump told reporters in West Palm Beach that he was "only kidding" and called it "just a little fun." He added that White likes the idea and is "a very capable guy." White is still due at the White House on Monday, and Trump said the meeting was planned for other reasons. The job has been empty since Katie Zacharia left in October after five days, and Karoline Leavitt had stepped down in August before her.

Why it matters

Trump still has no chief spokesperson at a time of turmoil after his party's heavy midterm losses and the replacement of his chief of staff, Susie Wiles, by Stephen Miller. Whoever gets the job will report to a new chief of staff and will face a Congress where Democrats are about to take control.
