Trump meets Qatar's mediator as Iran offers to restart talks if blockade lifted

November 9, 2026, updated 6:11 PM ET

President Trump was scheduled to meet Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani at the White House on Monday, November 9. The meeting was set to cover a new ceasefire and a restart of nuclear talks with Iran. In remarks aired Sunday, Trump told CBS's 60 Minutes there was "no reason" to escalate the war, and he told CNN that more US strikes were highly unlikely if talks made progress. Qatar denied reports that the talks had failed. On Monday, Iran's foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei defended a plan to resume negotiations within two days, on condition that Washington first lifts its naval blockade and sanctions. Secretary of State Marco Rubio called some of Iran's conditions unacceptable and said the two sides were not near a breakthrough.

Why it matters

The US blockade and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz are central to the war's effect on global energy supplies. Whether the Qatari mediation brings the two sides back to talks will decide whether the blockade is eased or the conflict escalates again. There was no sudden breakthrough. The change came from softer US language on escalation and new diplomatic activity.
