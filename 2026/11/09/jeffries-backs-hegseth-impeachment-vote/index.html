Jeffries says he will vote to impeach Hegseth as House vote nears

November 9, 2026, updated 6:11 PM ET

On ABC's "This Week" on Sunday, November 8, House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries said he would vote for the resolution to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. He accused Hegseth of abusing his power and of engineering the war with Iran, and said Democrats would discuss the resolution on Tuesday morning, when members return to Washington for the first time since the midterms. Republican Rep. Thomas Massie, who is leaving Congress, told ABC he plans to reintroduce a privileged resolution with eight articles of impeachment as early as Monday. That would require House leaders to schedule a vote within two legislative days. President Trump defended Hegseth the same day, saying he is guilty only of "winning two wars."

Why it matters

Jeffries is expected to become speaker after Democrats won the House last week. His support, together with Republican defections after the midterm losses, makes it more likely that a sitting defense secretary will be impeached during the lame-duck session and then face a Senate trial.
