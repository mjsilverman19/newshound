OpenAI starts selling Codex edition of ModRetro Chromatic handheld

November 9, 2026, updated 6:16 PM ET

On November 9 at about 18:48 UTC, OpenAI's developer account said the Codex edition of the ModRetro Chromatic, a handheld that plays Game Boy and Game Boy Color games, was on sale through OpenAI's Supply Co. store. A plugin for OpenAI's Codex coding agent lets buyers design levels, animate sprites and test their own games on the device. The handheld had earlier been given to DevDay attendees. The day before, at about 20:04 UTC on November 8, the account posted only "Tomorrow" with a video of a branded box, which set off guesses that a new model was coming. OpenAI has not announced any GPT-6.2 model, although some ChatGPT users have reported since November 8 that they are being routed to a model they believe is "GPT-6.2 Sol".

Why it matters

The sale takes OpenAI's pitch for building games with Codex beyond DevDay attendees to anyone who wants to buy the device. It also settles what the teaser was for. OpenAI's next frontier Astra model has still not been named or dated publicly since it cancelled GPT-6.1 Astra in September.
