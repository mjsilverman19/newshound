Judo champion Teddy Riner announces run for French president in 2027

November 9, 2026, updated 6:14 PM ET

Teddy Riner, France's five-time Olympic judo champion, announced on Monday, November 9, that he will run in the 2027 presidential election. He made the announcement in a video posted to social media around 10:35 UTC and in an interview with Le Parisien. Riner, 37 and still competing, presents himself as "neither right nor left." His campaign has a slogan, "La France qu'on veut," but no party behind it. President Emmanuel Macron, who cannot run again because of the two-term limit, welcomed his entry into the race. On the same day, prediction markets also moved on whether the US Federal Reserve will raise rates again in December. That shift came from a separate, slow drift after strong October jobs data, ahead of the November 10 inflation report.

Why it matters

Riner is one of France's best-known public figures. He joins an already crowded field in which far-right leader Marine Le Pen leads the polls, followed by Jean-Luc Mélenchon. To get on the ballot, he must collect 500 sponsorships from elected officials without a party organization behind him.
