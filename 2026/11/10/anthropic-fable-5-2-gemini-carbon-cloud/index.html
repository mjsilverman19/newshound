Gemini 4 Carbon billing listings and access appear on Google Cloud before any announcement

November 10, 2026, updated 9:22 AM ET

On November 10, billing SKUs for Gemini 4 Carbon turned up in Google Cloud's public catalog, OfficeChai reported. The model has been limited to vetted cyber defenders in Google's Fairwind Program since it was announced on October 28. The tracker StatsWire counted 90 Vertex AI SKUs covering online, batch and flex tiers. Online use is listed at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, double the $2 and $10 introductory rate. HuggingNews reported that calls from a new Google Cloud project using the "gemini-4-carbon" model name returned responses, and a developer posted on X at 08:07 UTC that the model was live on Vertex AI. Google has not confirmed a release date or any change in who can use Carbon.

Why it matters

If Carbon opens up as these listings suggest, Google's strongest model would move beyond a small cybersecurity pilot to general developers. That would come a day after Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 took over the top spot on a widely followed benchmark index. Gemini 4 Argon followed the same pattern a month earlier: it launched at the same introductory price and reached general availability about two weeks later.
