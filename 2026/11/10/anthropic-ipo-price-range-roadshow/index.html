Anthropic sets IPO price range of $70 to $78 and launches roadshow

November 10, 2026, updated 6:10 PM ET

Anthropic filed an amended registration statement on Tuesday, November 10, setting a price range of $70 to $78 a share for 825 million Class A shares and launching its IPO roadshow. Anthropic is selling about 801.4 million of the shares and existing holders about 23.6 million. That means a base raise of roughly $57.8 billion to $64.4 billion, plus a 30-day option for underwriters led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley to buy 123.75 million more shares. Separately, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC agreed to buy $26 billion of stock in a private placement at the IPO price. CNBC, citing people familiar with the plans, reported that the shares are expected to start trading on Nasdaq around November 19. Late on Monday, Reuters reported that Anthropic had emailed bankers a draft showing about 867 million shares at $70 to $80, then withdrawn it as inaccurate. The terms filed Tuesday are smaller than that draft.

Why it matters

This would be one of the largest IPOs ever, about five months after SpaceX's record listing. Even so, the implied valuation, about $1 trillion before dilution and up to about $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis, is well below the $2 trillion floated earlier this fall, so the deal is a test of investor appetite for AI after a weak stretch for tech stocks.
