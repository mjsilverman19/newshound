Van Hollen will not challenge Schumer for Senate majority leader, source says

November 5, 2026, updated 11:22 PM ET

Sen. Chris Van Hollen of Maryland does not plan to challenge Chuck Schumer for Senate majority leader. A source close to Van Hollen told NewsNation, and The Hill reported it on November 5. The source said Van Hollen wants the caucus to have an "honest and open conversation" about its agenda before it picks a leader. Earlier he had told Bloomberg that Democrats should not back Schumer or anyone else without a commitment to a "bold agenda." Schumer said on November 4 that he would be reelected leader. Several newly elected Democratic senators, including Abdul El-Sayed of Michigan, who had backed Van Hollen, have said they will not vote for Schumer.

Why it matters

Van Hollen was the alternative most often named by progressives. With him out, Schumer has no declared rival heading into next month's closed-door leadership vote for the new Democratic majority. The newcomers who oppose him still lack an alternative candidate.
