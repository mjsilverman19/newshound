Arab parties back Eisenkot, giving him a majority of recommendations for prime minister

November 5, 2026, updated 8:21 PM ET

On Thursday, the Joint List said it would unanimously recommend Yashar leader Gadi Eisenkot to President Isaac Herzog as prime minister, after Ra'am did the same. That gave Eisenkot 67 recommendations, a majority of the 120-seat Knesset, against 46 for Benjamin Netanyahu, whose total fell after United Torah Judaism declined to back anyone. Herzog's consultations closed on Thursday evening, and Eisenkot is expected to receive the mandate.

Why it matters

Eisenkot is now expected to get the first chance to form a government and end Netanyahu's time in office. The Arab parties backing him have not agreed to join a coalition, and some of his allies refuse to rely on them.
