Nigerian court voids Atiku's ADC presidential ticket and sacks party leadership

November 5, 2026, updated 5:40 PM ET

On Thursday, November 5, Justice Peter Lifu of the Federal High Court in Abuja ruled in a suit brought by ADC deputy national chairman Nafiu Bala Gombe. He nullified the David Mark-led leadership of the African Democratic Congress, including national secretary Rauf Aregbesola. The judge also cancelled every candidate the party nominated under that leadership, among them former Vice President Atiku Abubakar, its 2027 presidential candidate. The ADC and Atiku say they will go to the Court of Appeal. Peter Obi, now running for the Nigeria Democratic Congress, said voters, not courts, should decide who governs and called the ruling engineered by the ruling APC.

Why it matters

If the ruling stands, one of the main opposition candidates is off the January 16, 2027 ballot. Obi could then become the leading opposition challenger to President Tinubu. The ADC warns the ruling also puts its candidates for other offices at risk.
