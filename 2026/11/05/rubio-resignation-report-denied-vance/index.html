Rubio says he will serve out Trump's term and stop talking about 2028

November 5, 2026, updated 11:22 PM ET

On Thursday, November 5, Bloomberg News reported that Marco Rubio, who is both Secretary of State and national security adviser, was weighing whether to leave the Trump administration after Republicans lost ground in the midterms. The report said he had talked to White House officials about stepping down and been asked to stay. Rubio rejected the report later that day in a New York Times interview, carried by Reuters and AFP. He said working for Trump until the end of the term "has always been my plan" and added that he "literally will not talk about 2028 anymore." The State Department called the Bloomberg story made up and dismissed a separate Independent report that he was preparing a 2028 bid as baseless.

Why it matters

Rubio has been seen as the main possible Republican rival to Vice President JD Vance for 2028, and he has said before that he would back Vance if Vance runs. His commitment to stay in the cabinet through January 2029 leaves him little room to build a campaign, which makes Vance look more clearly like the front-runner, though Rubio has not ruled out a run in so many words.
