Final New Zealand poll puts Labour ahead, with Opportunity holding the balance

November 5, 2026, updated 11:22 PM ET

The final The Post/Freshwater Strategy poll before New Zealand's November 7 election, published on November 5 and 6 local time, put Labour on 30.7% and National on 26.4%. Labour was up two points and National down 1.2. The Greens fell to 13%. NZ First rose to 12% and ACT to 9.8%, while the Opportunity Party sat just over the 5% threshold at 5.1%, where The Post says it would hold the balance of power. Earlier polls this week, including the final 1News Verian poll, showed the race neck and neck. The day's shifts across New Zealand and Spain do not point to one trigger: they look like a drift driven by late polling. In Spain, new polls on November 5, including SW Demoscopia, had the PSOE narrowly first but PP and Vox together still on course for a slim majority ahead of the November 29 vote.

Why it matters

Labour now leads in this poll, but the National-led bloc's partners held steady, so the result probably comes down to whether Opportunity clears 5% and which side it backs. That makes Saturday's vote a toss-up rather than a clear change of government. In Spain, the new polls do little to change the picture of a right-leaning majority led by Feijóo.
