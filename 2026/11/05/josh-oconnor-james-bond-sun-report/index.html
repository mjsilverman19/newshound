Tabloid report says Josh O'Connor told castmates he will be the next James Bond

November 5, 2026, updated 8:34 PM ET

On Thursday, November 5, The Sun quoted an unnamed source who said they met one of Josh O'Connor's castmates from the West End play Golden Boy at The French House, a London pub. According to the source, the castmate said O'Connor had told the cast he had won the James Bond role when they had drinks after the play's last night on Halloween. Page Six, NME and others followed up the report. Page Six asked Amazon and EON Productions for comment, and the studio has made no announcement. Earlier that week O'Connor told the BBC he was "very honored" to be considered and would say nothing more. Reports in October named him, Callum Turner and Jack Lowden among the actors in final screen tests for Denis Villeneuve's Bond 26.

Why it matters

This is the most specific claim yet that the casting has been decided, and it makes O'Connor the reported frontrunner over Callum Turner. But it rests on one secondhand pub source, and nothing is confirmed until Amazon MGM announces its choice. Deadline has said that could come before Thanksgiving.
