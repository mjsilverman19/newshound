Anthropic IPO meets valuation pushback as AI stocks slide after midterms

November 5, 2026, updated 11:22 PM ET

Tech and AI stocks sold off hard on Thursday, November 5, as investors reacted to Democrats' wide wins in the midterms. CBS News reported the Dow fell more than 1,800 points, with chipmakers and AI names such as Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom and Micron leading the declines. The Wall Street Journal reported, as relayed by Investing.com, that Anthropic is facing pushback on a valuation of about $1.5 trillion for its planned November listing, and that executives and advisers will meet in the coming days to review the offering. On the same day SpaceX, which listed in June at about $1.75 trillion, was pushing ahead with Starlink Mobile after announcing a 5G network deal with Qualcomm on November 4. There is no sign of a single SpaceX-specific trigger; the shift mostly reflects the market rout's effect on Anthropic.

Why it matters

Anthropic had been aiming for a debut before Thanksgiving at a valuation big enough to overtake SpaceX as the largest listing of 2026. A lower price or another delay makes that less likely and could leave SpaceX's June IPO as the year's largest.
