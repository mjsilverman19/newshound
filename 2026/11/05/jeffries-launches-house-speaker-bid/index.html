Jeffries formally launches bid for House speaker after Democrats win majority

November 5, 2026, updated 11:22 PM ET

On Wednesday, November 4, House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries of New York formally asked for his colleagues' support to become the next Speaker of the House. He did it in a "Dear Colleague" letter, first reported by Axios, that set out three priorities: affordability, accountability and American values. The letter came the day after Democrats won control of the House by a wide margin in the midterm elections, with the AP calling well over the 218 seats needed. Progressive leaders had already said they would back him, and no serious challenger has come forward, though an online petition urging Democrats to pick someone else drew wide attention on November 5.

Why it matters

With a large majority and no organized opposition inside the caucus, Jeffries looks very likely to win the gavel on the first ballot in January and become the first Black speaker. That would end Republican control of the House agenda under Mike Johnson.
