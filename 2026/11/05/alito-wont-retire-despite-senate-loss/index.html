Alito tells friends he won't retire now despite Democrats winning the Senate

November 5, 2026, updated 11:22 PM ET

On Wednesday evening, November 4, The New York Times reported that Justice Samuel Alito, 76, had told people close to him he does not plan to retire right away. That was a day after Democrats won control of the Senate, which leaves Republicans only the lame-duck session before January 3 to confirm a successor. Bloomberg confirmed the report through a person who spoke with the justice on Wednesday. CNN said several sources close to both Alito and Clarence Thomas, 78, did not expect either justice to leave the court because of the election.

Why it matters

These reports close off the most talked-about route for President Trump to fill a fourth Supreme Court seat. Once Democrats hold the Senate, they can block any nominee, so a vacancy during the rest of his term now looks unlikely. Alito did say before the election that he plans to stay through the current term, which ends in June.
