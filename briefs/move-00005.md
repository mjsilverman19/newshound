## Assessment

The drop looks like the market unwinding a spike driven by The Independent's report (circulating by ~14:00 UTC) that Rubio may resign to run in 2028. The White House (Davis Ingle) and State Department (Dylan Johnson) flatly denied it; Johnson's X denial was posted at 16:00 UTC, about five minutes before the move began, and earlier press pieces already carried the denials. The move is well explained by those official denials.
