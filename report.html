How stories are ranked

Every hour Newshound compares the prices of about 55,000 Polymarket and Kalshi markets with an hour earlier. Markets that move 5 points or more with real money behind them become stories, grouped by the event they are about.

A story's importance is the money that repriced on it: for each market, the points it moved times the dollars traded during the move, weighted up when trading ran far above its usual pace. The index is on a log scale, so every 10 points means ten times as much. Stories scoring 30 or more are investigated; the rest are listed with their numbers.

The ranking reflects what traders bet on. Politics, sports and economics carry the most money, and news without a market behind it doesn't appear.

State Department and White House deny report that Rubio is preparing to resign

Officials called The Independent's report that Rubio could quit to prepare a 2028 run fabricated, and traders cut the odds he leaves this year.

Importance 39 · $6K traded · 1 market · 24 pts · moving since Nov 5, 11:30 AM ET

On Thursday morning, The Independent reported, citing anonymous sources in Washington and the UK government, that Secretary of State Marco Rubio was on resignation watch after the Republican midterm losses and could leave to prepare a 2028 presidential bid. The White House (spokesperson Davis Ingle) and the State Department (Assistant Secretary Dylan Johnson) rejected it, with Johnson calling it completely baseless fake news. Sergio Gor, named as a possible replacement, also called the story nonsense.

Why the money moved

The odds Rubio leaves before 2027 had been lifted by the report, and fell from 58% to 34% from about 11:30 ET as the official denials spread and further coverage led with them.

Eisenkot nears Israel's mandate as Herzog's consultations close with 67 recommendations

With a majority of the Knesset behind him, Gadi Eisenkot is expected to get the first chance to form a government, leaving Netanyahu at 46.

Importance 38 · $26K traded · 2 markets · 6 pts · moving since Nov 5, 12:36 PM ET

President Isaac Herzog's two days of consultations on who should form Israel's next government wound up on Thursday evening, November 5, with Eisenkot's Yashar! and Netanyahu's Likud meeting him last. After Ra'am and the Joint List backed him on Thursday, Eisenkot had 67 recommendations against 46 for Netanyahu, with United Torah Judaism recommending no one. Yashar! told Herzog it wants an emergency government and said Netanyahu would become a caretaker prime minister. Eisenkot is expected to receive the mandate once the consultations end.

Why the money moved

A mandate for Eisenkot backed by a Knesset majority makes it more likely Netanyahu leaves office. Kalshi's market on Eisenkot rose from about 12:36 ET and Polymarket's 'Netanyahu out by end of 2026' went from 24% to 30% from 12:56 ET, as reports from the final evening meetings said Eisenkot was nearing the mandate.
